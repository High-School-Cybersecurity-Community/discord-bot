# Outback Overflow '26:

Outback Overflow 26 is a free, online, 24-hour capture the flag competition for high schoolers across Australia. It runs on CTFd from 10:00 AEDT Saturday 21 November to 10:00 AEDT Sunday 22 November 2026. Anyone can play, and high school teams can compete for their own prizes.

CTF Instance Active at https://ctf.hscyber.org/

Register at https://ctf.hscyber.org/register/

For more information: https://oo26.hscyber.org/
